    Why nurse resumes fail ATS — and what hospitals search for

    Hospital ATS systems (Taleo, Workday, iCIMS, Oracle HCM, HealthStream) use very structured keyword matching for nursing. Licensure (RN, BSN, NCLEX), unit type (ICU, ED, Med-Surg) and EHR system name (Epic, Cerner) are near-universal filters. Missing even 3–5 of these can drop your score below the recruiter's filter threshold.

    Top keywords this checker looks for

    RN, BSN, NCLEX, Patient Care, Clinical Assessment, Medication Administration, IV Therapy, Wound Care, EHR, Epic, Cerner, Meditech, BLS, ACLS, PALS, Triage, Discharge Planning, Patient Education, Care Coordination, ICU, Critical Care, Telemetry, Charge Nurse, HIPAA.

    Nurse-specific mistakes to avoid

    • Licence written only as an abbreviation: write “Registered Nurse (RN)” — write both forms. The abbreviation alone can miss full-phrase searches.
    • No unit named: “ICU / Med-Surg / ED” — recruiters search by unit. Name yours exactly.
    • No EHR named: Epic or Cerner experience is a real keyword, especially during system migrations.
    • Certifications buried in a text box: BLS and ACLS must appear as standard searchable text, not in a graphic or table.
    • No numbers: add patient ratios, e.g. “cared for 5–6 patients per shift in a 24-bed ICU”, and measurable outcomes.
    • Missing shift detail: add licence line, specialty keywords, shift and unit detail, and formatting a hospital ATS can read.

    Benchmark: the average nurse resume in our sample scores 52. A score of 75+ puts you well ahead of most applicants for the same posting.

    Frequently asked questions — nurses

    Do hospitals really use ATS for nurses?

    Yes. Large US hospital systems use Taleo, Workday, iCIMS and similar systems to screen the first round of nurse applications by licence, certifications, unit experience and EHR system.

    Should I list both RN and Registered Nurse?

    Yes. Write “Registered Nurse (RN)” the first time, then use RN afterwards. That way both the full phrase and the abbreviation are searchable.

    Is my resume stored anywhere?

    No. Checking happens only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
