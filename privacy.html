Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Your resume stays in your browser

When you paste resume text or upload a PDF, the text is processed only in your browser tab, on your device. It is not uploaded to our server, stored, shared, or sold. There is no user account and no login, so we do not collect your resume as part of an account.

What we do collect

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (for example Cloudflare Web Analytics and Google Analytics) to count visits, see which pages are popular, and understand which countries visitors come from. Those tools may collect anonymised usage data such as pages visited, device type and approximate location. They do not receive your resume text.

Cookies and advertising

We do not currently run third-party ads on this site. If advertising (for example Google AdSense) is added in future, third-party vendors may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at any time in your browser settings or via Google Ads Settings. This policy will be updated if that changes.

Contact

If you contact us via the Contact page, we will use your message only to reply to you. We do not sell contact details.

Children

This site is intended for job seekers aged 18 and over.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
