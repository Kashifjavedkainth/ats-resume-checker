    Why accountant resumes fail ATS — and what finance teams search for

    Finance ATS systems (Workday, Taleo, iCIMS, ADP, Oracle HCM) screen accountant applications by certification (CPA), accounting standard (GAAP), software (QuickBooks, SAP, Oracle) and close experience. A resume that says 'did accounting' scores far lower than one that says 'Closed month-end in 3 days, reconciled 200+ accounts and cut reporting errors by 25%'. Name your certification, your software and your measurable results.

    Top keywords this checker looks for

    CPA, GAAP, QuickBooks, Microsoft Excel, Financial Reporting, Auditing, Tax Preparation, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, General Ledger, Budgeting, Month-End Close, Payroll, Financial Analysis, Internal Controls, Reconciliation, Bookkeeping, ERP, SAP, Oracle, Cost Accounting, Forecasting, Compliance, Variance Analysis.

    Accountant mistakes to avoid

    • Hiding your CPA status: put 'CPA' or 'CPA candidate' at the top with the state. Recruiters filter by CPA first — if the parser cannot find it, you are out.
    • No software named: QuickBooks, SAP, Oracle and ERP are real keywords. Name every system you have actually used, with your level of experience.
    • Duties without numbers: 'handled accounts payable' scores badly. 'Processed 500+ accounts payable invoices per month with 99% accuracy' scores well.
    • Forgetting month-end close: month-end close, reconciliation and general ledger are core filters. If you have done them, say how fast and how accurately.
    • No tax or audit detail: tax preparation, auditing and compliance are searched separately. Name the tax types and audit work you have done.
    • A resume with no dates: every role and degree needs years. Missing dates make the parser — and the recruiter — distrust the timeline.

    Benchmark: the average accountant resume in our sample scores 57. A score of 75+ puts you well ahead of most applicants for the same posting.

    Frequently asked questions — accountants

    Do I need a CPA to pass ATS for accounting jobs?

    Not always — many staff accountant roles accept a bachelor's degree plus QuickBooks and Excel experience. But if you have a CPA, put it at the top: it is one of the strongest keywords in accounting.

    Should I list QuickBooks and Excel separately?

    Yes. QuickBooks and Microsoft Excel are searched as separate keywords, so name both and say what you did with each one.

    Is my resume stored anywhere?

    No. Checking happens only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
