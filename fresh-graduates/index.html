    How to write a graduate resume that passes ATS — with no experience

    Put Education first, before Experience. Recruiters and ATS systems expect a graduate resume to lead with your degree, university, graduation year, GPA (only if 3.5 or above), relevant coursework and honours.

    What to use instead of work experience

    • Internships: even a 6-week internship counts. Give it the same format as a job — title, company, dates, 3 bullets with numbers.
    • University projects: name the project, the tools you used, and the result. Example: “Built a customer survey dashboard in Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets for 200 responses; presented findings to a class of 40.”
    • Coursework: list only courses that match the job — e.g. Marketing Research, Data Analysis, Financial Accounting — not every course you took.
    • Part-time and campus jobs: retail, tutoring, campus ambassador and volunteering all show teamwork, communication and time management.
    • Skills section: put the exact tools from the job posting — Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Canva, SQL, Python, customer service — if you can actually use them.

    Top keywords this checker looks for

    Internship, University Projects, Coursework, Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, Data Analysis, Communication, Teamwork, Problem Solving, Time Management, Research, Presentation, Customer Service, Social Media, Canva, SQL, Python, Adaptability, Leadership, Volunteering, Academic Projects, Relevant Coursework.

    Common fresher mistakes

    • Leaving Experience blank: do not leave it empty — use internships, projects and part-time work instead.
    • Listing high school: once you have a university degree, remove high school. It wastes space and dates you.
    • No numbers: “helped with a project” scores badly. “Surveyed 200 students and increased club membership by 30%” scores well.
    • Objective instead of Summary: write a 2-line summary about what you can do for the employer, not what job you want.
    • Copying the job posting word for word: use the keywords, but describe your real experience in your own words. Recruiters can tell.
    • One page is enough: a graduate resume should be one page. Two pages with no experience looks empty.

    Frequently asked questions — fresh graduates

    I have no experience. Can I still pass ATS?

    Yes. ATS scores keywords and formatting, not years of experience alone. Internships, projects, coursework and skills can match most of the keywords in an entry-level posting.

    Should Education go first?

    Yes, for a fresh graduate. Put Education at the top, then Projects / Internships, then Skills. Move Experience to the top only after you have 1–2 years of full-time work.

    Is my resume stored anywhere?

    No. Checking happens only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
