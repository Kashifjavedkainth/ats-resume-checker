    Why data analyst resumes fail ATS — and what hiring managers search for

    Analyst ATS systems (Greenhouse, Workday, Lever, iCIMS, Ashby) screen applications by tool (SQL, Python, Tableau, Power BI), technique (statistics, A/B testing, data cleaning) and business impact. A resume that says 'worked with data' scores far lower than one that says 'Built a Tableau dashboard in SQL and Python that cut weekly reporting time by 50%'. Name your tools, your techniques and the decision your work changed.

    Top keywords this checker looks for

    SQL, Python, Tableau, Power BI, Data Visualization, Microsoft Excel, Statistics, A/B Testing, Data Cleaning, Data Analysis, Business Intelligence, Dashboard Design, ETL, R, Pandas, Data Modeling, Reporting, Forecasting, Google Analytics, Machine Learning, Data Warehousing, Snowflake, Communication, Problem Solving.

    Data analyst mistakes to avoid

    • Tools without a business result: 'used SQL and Tableau' scores badly. 'Built a Tableau dashboard from SQL data that cut reporting time by 50%' scores well.
    • No statistics or testing named: statistics, A/B testing and forecasting are searched separately from SQL. Name the techniques you actually used.
    • Hiding your dashboards: put your portfolio and dashboard links as plain text near the top. Links hidden in icons or images are invisible to the parser.
    • Forgetting data cleaning: data cleaning and ETL are core analyst keywords. If you spent half your time cleaning data, say so.
    • One generic resume for every role: a business intelligence job and a product analytics job need different keyword emphasis. Tailor the top skills and project bullets for each posting.
    • Too much jargon, no story: 'leveraged synergies' means nothing to an ATS. Replace buzzwords with tools, techniques and numbers.

    Benchmark: the average data analyst resume in our sample scores 56. A score of 75+ puts you well ahead of most applicants for the same posting.

    Frequently asked questions — data analysts

    Do I need Python and SQL both?

    Most US data analyst postings search for SQL first and Python second. If you know both, name both and show a project for each one — SQL for querying, Python with Pandas for analysis.

    Should I include my Tableau or Power BI dashboards?

    Yes — as plain text links near the top of your resume, with 2–3 named dashboards. The ATS cannot click icons or read images, so the full URL must be written as text.

    Is my resume stored anywhere?

    No. Checking happens only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
