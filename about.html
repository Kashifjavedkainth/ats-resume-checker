About ATS Resume Checker

ATS Resume Checker is a free tool built for job seekers in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada and Europe. Most large employers now filter resumes with an Applicant Tracking System (ATS) — software from companies like Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo, iCIMS and Lever — before a human recruiter ever reads them.

Many good candidates are rejected by that software, not because they are unqualified, but because their resume does not use the keywords the job posting asks for, or because its formatting cannot be read by the parser.

This site was built to fix that, in a simple way: paste your resume, paste the job description, and see in 30 seconds what the ATS is likely to see — your score, the keywords you matched, the keywords you missed, and the formatting to fix.

Why it is different

  • Role-specific checkers: a nurse resume is not scored like a graduate resume. Our Nurses and Fresh Graduates pages use different keywords, different sample jobs and different guidance for that audience.
  • Private by design: all checking happens in your browser. Your resume is never uploaded to a server, stored, or sold.
  • Free, no signup: no account, no credit card, no email required to run a check.

What this tool is not

Scores are an estimate to help you improve your resume. They are not a guarantee of an interview or a job, and they do not replace a human recruiter's judgement. Always add a keyword only if it is true for you.
