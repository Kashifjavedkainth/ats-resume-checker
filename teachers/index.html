    Why teacher resumes fail ATS — and what districts search for

    District ATS systems (Frontline, AppliTrack, Taleo, Workday, SchoolSpring) screen teacher applications by teaching license, grade level, subject and curriculum keywords. A resume that says 'taught kids' scores far lower than one that says 'Taught Grade 3 reading with differentiated instruction, raising reading scores by 18%'. Name your licence, your grade, your tools — Google Classroom, IEP, state standards — and your measurable results.

    Top keywords this checker looks for

    Teaching License, Classroom Management, Lesson Planning, Curriculum Development, IEP, Special Education, Google Classroom, State Standards, Differentiated Instruction, Student Assessment, Parent Communication, Behavior Management, Elementary Education, Reading Instruction, Math Instruction, ESL, Professional Development, Collaboration, Data-Driven Instruction, Literacy, STEM, Substitute Teaching, CPR, First Aid.

    Teacher mistakes to avoid

    • Hiding your teaching license: put your teaching license, state and expiry at the top. Districts filter by licence first — if the parser cannot find it, you are out.
    • No grade or subject named: 'teacher' is too vague. Write 'Grade 3 Elementary Education teacher, Reading Instruction and Math Instruction' so the search matches.
    • No measurable student results: 'helped students learn' scores badly. 'Raised Grade 3 reading scores by 18% with differentiated instruction' scores well.
    • Forgetting IEP and special education: if you have IEP or special education experience, name it. Districts search for it constantly and it is a rare keyword.
    • No tools listed: Google Classroom, student assessment tools and data-driven instruction are real keywords. List the tools you actually use.
    • A resume longer than two pages: keep a teacher resume to 1–2 pages. Long resumes with no numbers look padded to both the ATS and the principal.

    Frequently asked questions — teachers

    Do school districts really use ATS?

    Yes. Most large US districts use Frontline, AppliTrack or Taleo to screen the first round of teacher applications by licence, grade, subject and certifications.

    Should I include my teaching license number?

    Include the licence type, state and subject — e.g. 'Colorado Teaching License, Elementary Education K–6'. You do not need to print the full licence number on a public resume.

    Is my resume stored anywhere?

    No. Checking happens only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
