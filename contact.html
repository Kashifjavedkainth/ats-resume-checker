Contact us

Questions about your ATS score, feedback on the checker, or a request for a new role-specific checker (for example teachers, data analysts or accountants)? We would like to hear from you.

Email: contact@atsresumechecker.example — please replace with the site's real contact email when the custom domain is live. Until then, use the contact form on the live site once deployed, or reach out via the site owner's GitHub profile: Kashifjavedkainth.

What to include

  • Which page you used (main checker, Nurses, or Fresh Graduates)
  • What you expected and what happened
  • Do not send your full resume by email — run the check on the site instead, where your resume stays in your browser.

We aim to reply within a few working days. This is a free tool, so support is best-effort.
