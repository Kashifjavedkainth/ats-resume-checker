    Why marketing resumes fail ATS — and what recruiters search for

    Marketing ATS systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, JazzHR, BambooHR) screen applications by channel (SEO, social media, email marketing), tool (HubSpot, Google Analytics, Google Ads) and measurable campaign results. A resume that says 'did social media' scores far lower than one that says 'Grew Instagram engagement by 45% and generated 1,200 leads with a HubSpot email campaign'. Name your channels, your tools and your numbers.

    Top keywords this checker looks for

    SEO, Content Marketing, Google Analytics, Social Media Marketing, Email Marketing, HubSpot, Campaign Management, Digital Marketing, Brand Management, PPC, Google Ads, Marketing Automation, Copywriting, Market Research, Lead Generation, Conversion Rate Optimization, A/B Testing, WordPress, Canva, Adobe Creative Suite, Influencer Marketing, Analytics, CRM, Marketing Strategy.

    Marketing mistakes to avoid

    • Channels without numbers: 'managed social media' scores badly. 'Grew LinkedIn followers by 60% and generated 1,200 leads' scores well.
    • No tools named: HubSpot, Google Analytics, Google Ads and WordPress are real keywords. List every tool you have actually used.
    • Hiding your portfolio: put your portfolio and campaign links as plain text near the top. Links hidden in icons or images are invisible to the parser.
    • Forgetting SEO and analytics: SEO, analytics and conversion rate optimization are searched separately from social media. Name them all if you do them.
    • One generic resume for every role: an SEO job and a social media job need different keyword emphasis. Tailor the top skills and campaign bullets for each posting.
    • Buzzwords instead of results: 'results-driven marketer' means nothing to an ATS. Replace buzzwords with channels, tools and numbers.

    Benchmark: the average marketing resume in our sample scores 54. A score of 75+ puts you well ahead of most applicants for the same posting.

    Frequently asked questions — marketing professionals

    Should I include my portfolio link?

    Yes — as plain text near the top of your resume, with 2–3 named campaigns. The ATS cannot click icons or read images, so the full URL must be written as text.

    Is social media experience enough for a marketing job?

    It helps, but most postings also search for SEO, email marketing, Google Analytics and campaign management. Show at least two channels plus a tool like HubSpot.

    Is my resume stored anywhere?

    No. Checking happens only in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
